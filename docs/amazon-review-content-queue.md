## Draft-Ready Reviews

### Fiebing's Black Leather Dye, Pack of 2

```text
This is a regular buy for my leatherwork. Out of the black leather dyes I have used, this is one of the best penetrating black dyes I have found. It gives a strong, deep black and holds up well as long as the leather is sealed properly after dyeing.

The main warning is that it is messy if you are careless with it. Do not spill it on your workspace unless you want permanent Dalmatian spots on your bench. Gloves and scrap testing are a good idea.

I use this for real leather projects and will keep buying it. My only complaint is that I wish it were easier to get in larger quantities.
```

### ANYCUBIC PLA Plus 1.75mm Gray Filament

```text
I have had good results with this PLA+ filament, especially for the price. I use it for 3D printed embossing stamp prototypes and shop tooling tests.

My usual settings are around 215 degrees C nozzle temperature and 65 degrees C bed temperature, running a 0.6 mm nozzle. It prints solidly, holds detail well enough for prototype leather stamps, and has been reliable for my workflow.

I would not call printed PLA+ a replacement for brass or metal tooling, but for testing stamp designs and making prototype embossing tools, this filament has worked well for me.
```

### Angelus Leather Dye, Green

```text
I use Angelus leather dyes regularly, and this green is one I am happy with. It works well for leather projects when the leather is prepped properly and applied with care.

Like most leather dye, the final result depends heavily on the leather, prep, and sealing afterward. I always recommend testing on scrap first, especially with brighter colors.

Overall, Angelus dyes have been dependable for my leatherwork, and I would buy this color again.
```

### Angelus Leather Dye, Yellow

```text
I use Angelus leather dyes regularly, and this yellow gets my approval. It is a good option when I need a brighter leather color instead of the usual browns, blacks, and darker tones.

As with any leather dye, test it on scrap first. The leather type, prep, and number of coats can change the final look quite a bit. Once applied and sealed properly, it works well for my leather projects.

I would buy it again.
```

### Angelus Leather Dye, Purple

```text
I have had good results with Angelus leather dyes, and this purple fits the same standard. It is useful when I want a stronger color option for custom leather pieces instead of sticking to traditional leather tones.

Prep and sealing matter. I would not skip scrap testing, especially with colors like purple where the final shade can vary depending on the leather.

Overall, I am happy with it and would buy it again.
```

### NFC Stickers / NTAG215 Pack

```text
These NFC stickers have worked well for me. I programmed them from my phone using the NFC Tools app, and the process was simple.

They are easy to set up and useful for linking physical items to digital information. For my use, they have been reliable and straightforward.

Good option if you need basic programmable NFC tags and want something simple to work with from a phone.
```
