## Best first-wave targets

### 1. Weaver Leather Supply

Why it fits:

- strong leathercraft audience overlap
- clear maker-facing content program
- visible maker ecosystem already on site
- natural fit for shop workflow, stamps, tools, and leather build education

Why to be careful:

- the Featured Maker media terms are broad and brand-favoring
- affiliate payout details were not public

Best move:

- separate `maker/content visibility` from `affiliate cash terms`
- ask for affiliate terms before committing

### 2. Angelus Direct

Why it fits:

- strong dye / finish / paint lane
- visible public partnership language
- content categories line up with leather color testing, finish comparisons, and restoration use

Why to be careful:

- public page does not prove commission or cookie terms
- wholesale path is brick-and-mortar only

Best move:

- use creator / referral / content lane, not wholesale

### 3. District Leather Supply

Why it fits:

- direct overlap with CNC cutting, splitting, and strap production
- good Horizon fit for scaling from prototype to repeatable parts
- feels relationship-driven rather than corporate

Best move:

- direct maker-to-maker contact around services and workflow content

### 4. Rocky Mountain Leather Supply

Why it fits:

- broad leather + tool + hardware fit
- public wholesale discounts
- visible samples, services, and machine-adjacent product lanes

Best move:

- treat as supplier/service relationship first

### 5. Buckleguy

Why it fits:

- excellent hardware fit for collars, straps, journals, bags, and buckle content
- public rewards/referral lane exists

Why it is less exciting:

- no public cash affiliate structure found
- points/rewards program is useful, but not the same as a real commission lane

## Park / secondary lanes

- `Tandy Leather`: valuable supplier and education lane, but not a proven creator/affiliate lane from public pages.
- `Montana Leather Company`: useful wholesale/sample supplier lane, but tax/business details make it a manual-only follow-up.
- `Barry King Tools`: excellent premium-tool review target, but likely direct outreach only.
- `C.S. Osborne`: legacy tool maker, likely direct outreach only.
- `Fiebing's`: extremely strong product fit, but looks like a direct relationship pitch rather than an application flow.

## Suggested next actions

1. Draft direct outreach for `Weaver`, `Angelus`, `Fiebing's`, `District Leather Supply`, and `Rocky Mountain Leather Supply`.
2. Keep `Buckleguy` on a hardware-first relationship lane, not a heavy affiliate-money lane.
3. Treat `Montana Leather` wholesale and `Tandy` commercial paths as Curtis-manual if pursued because business/tax details may be required.
4. For `Barry King` and `C.S. Osborne`, use a premium-tool review and honest bench-use angle instead of asking for generic influencer treatment.
