## 5. Allowed copy style

Use wording like:

- `AtomStack's current affiliate promo materials list this offer as...`
- `Current Impact promo asset says...`
- `AtomStack currently lists code AFFGlacier270 for...`
- `According to the current affiliate asset, this offer is not combinable with other store discounts.`

Tone rules:

- keep it factual
- keep it time-bound
- keep it source-bound to current promo assets
- separate supplier marketing claims from Horizon's own tested experience

## 6. Do not say

- Do not present AtomStack marketing claims as Horizon-tested facts unless Curtis actually tested the machine.
- Do not improvise stronger savings numbers than the current asset shows.
- Do not imply discounts stack when the asset says they do not.
- Do not claim paid search, trademark bidding, or email promotion is allowed without proof.
- Do not write product-comparison claims like `better than X` or `beats Y` without tested evidence and policy clearance.
- Do not phrase affiliate copy like a sponsor endorsement if this is only an affiliate relationship.
